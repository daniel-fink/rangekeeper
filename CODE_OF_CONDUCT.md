# Code of conduct

Rangekeeper adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to
anyone who represents the project in public.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in each review and discussion.
- Give and accept technical criticism about the work, not about the person.
- Do not harass, insult or threaten anyone.
- Do not publish private information about another person without permission.

## Report a problem

Send conduct reports by email to the maintainer, Daniel Fink, at
<danfink@mit.edu>. Do not report a conduct problem in a public issue. The
maintainer reviews each report, keeps the reporter's identity private and
replies with the action taken.

Report security vulnerabilities through the [security policy](SECURITY.md), not
through this channel.

## Enforcement

The maintainer applies the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant:
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, according to the impact of
the behavior.
