The model calls that took one positional subject or UUID now take every argument by name (D14, strict reading): `Model.entity(id=)`, `Model.value(id=)`, `Model.label(id=)`, `ModelDraft.entity(id=)`, `Model.from_data(data=, units=)`, `View(model=, ...)`, `View.quantities(key=, units=)`, `Hierarchy(view=, membership_root=)`, `Hierarchy.from_membership(view=, root=)`, `QuantityRollup.pivot` and `FlowRollup.pivot(hierarchy=, ...)`, `FlowRollup.formulate(hierarchy=, id=, aggregates=)`, `select_value(key=, measure=)`, `to_table(source=, ...)`, `match(model=, entities=, condition=)`, the `rangekeeper.model.formulation` builders (`flow.sum(model=, id=, ...)` and the others), the `rangekeeper.model.definitions` lookups (`measure(items=, id=)`), `provenance.fact_for(model=, target=)` and `locations(model=, claim_id=)`, and `expression.text.parse(text=, names=)` and `render(expression=, name=)`. The records `Token` and `Spelling` take keyword arguments only. In `rk.market.dynamics`, `calculate_autoregression(innovations=, ...)`, `accumulate_volatility(trend=, returns=, ...)` and `calculate_shock(events=, ...)` do the same. Name the arguments that you passed by position; one-argument lookups such as `View.entity(id)` and `RollupResult.value(id)` stay positional.
