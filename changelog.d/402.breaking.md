`rk.duration.Frequency.BIWEEK` is removed, with its alignment rules in `containing_period`. `offset`, `count_steps`, `make_periods`, `Periods`, `Span.periods` and `Flow.resample` no longer accept a fortnight. For a 14-day step, use `offset(..., frequency=Frequency.WEEK, count=2)`; for a 14-day grid, give the Periods explicitly.
