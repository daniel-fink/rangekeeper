Execution runs as typed stages, each a public function in `rangekeeper.execution` that writes to no store: `resolve_cases`, `validate`, `bind_roles`, `build_problem`, the backend's `solve`, `make_candidate`, `verify` and `assemble`. The internal names change: `execution.planning.Plan` is replaced by `resolve_cases` and `Cases`; `preparation.prepare` by `validate` then `bind_roles` (`Prepared` is `Binding`); `compiler.compile` by `build_problem` (`Compiled` is `AffineProblem`, and its `prepared` field is `binding`); `publication.candidate` by `make_candidate`; and `acceptance.check` by `verify` (`Acceptance` is `Verification`). The codemod applies the renames.
