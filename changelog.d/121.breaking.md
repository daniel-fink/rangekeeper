The stored query `Filter` changes. Its `classification` and each Label `Criterion` now match sub-types in the Taxonomy unless the new `exclusive` field is true, and a Filter has a new `tests` list. `tools/upgrade` writes `exclusive: true` when it converts 0.x documents, and documents and Bundles that 1.0.0a2 wrote (`--from 1.0.0a2`, #386), so they keep their exact match.
`Criterion` moves from `rangekeeper.model.expression` to `rangekeeper.model`, beside `Filter` and `ValueTest`; the codemod moves the import.
