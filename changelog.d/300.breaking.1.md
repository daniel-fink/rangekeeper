`rk.execution.DecisionResult` is generated from the schema, and `DecisionResult.of` is gone: a stored `Check` records its result. `Report.rule_evidence` now holds one `RuleEvidence`, the decision trace of a case. The evidence of the dated `RuleSet` slice is now `DecisionEvidence` in `Report.decision_evidence`, and the conversion tool writes `outcomes` to that field.
