A growth profile of an amount per unit of time, such as `initial="1e6 AUD pa"`, gives each Period that amount times the Period's length: a Flow in `AUD`, with a year's amount in a yearly Period and a quarter of it in a quarterly Period. `rx.profile.*` gives the same units, so lesson 03's `a_rent` in `AUD` is a profile of `a_income` in `AUD pa`. A plain amount does not change; a distribution of a total per unit of time raises `UnitError`.
