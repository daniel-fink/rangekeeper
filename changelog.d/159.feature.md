`QuantityRollup.pivot` and `FlowRollup.pivot` take `label=`: the columns test an Entity's Label with that key instead of its Classification. Without `by=`, the columns start at the root of the one Taxonomy that the Labels use. `Pivot.partition` reports Entities in several label columns as `overlap` and Entities without the Label as `uncovered`; `Pivot.label` records the key.
