`rk.execution.solve` and `execute` run the rules of a Specification, on each path by itself, with one result in any number of batch workers. `outcome.decisions` gives the decision traces in a `DecisionTable`, with `where(key=, result=)` and `observations`, and `outcome.case(key=).trace` gives the trace of one case. `run.rule_evidence` gives the stored trace and the result of its check. `rk.run.validate` checks the trace of a stored Run, and `rk.execution.replay` compares traces and names the first window date that differs.
