`rk.model` calls take keyword arguments only (D14): the `Pivot` lookups (`pivot.value(row=, column=)`, and `available_value`, `cell`, `coverage` and `partition`), `Pivot(rows=, columns=)`, `classified(model=, classification=)`, `declare(id=, name=, equations=, values=)`, the `membership` functions and `formulation.flow.shape` and `aligned`. The records `Coverage`, `RollupEntry`, `RollupResult`, `Match`, `PivotColumn`, `Partition`, `FieldColumn`, `ValueColumn`, `LabelColumn` and `PropertyColumn` take keyword arguments only. Name each argument; the upgrade guide lists the calls.
