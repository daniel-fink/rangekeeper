`specification`, `model` and `Outcome` raise Rangekeeper errors with a code, not plain `ValueError`, `TypeError` or `KeyError`, for failures that are not a wrong Python type. A batch where one case is needed, or a leaf where a batch is needed, raises `ContractError` with the new codes `batch.unsupported` and `batch.required`: `Outcome.case` on a leaf, and `model`, `evidence`, `free_unknowns`, `stream` and `outcome[key]` on a batch, raised `TypeError` before; catch `ContractError` or `ValueError` now. A Specification without its Model or resolver raises `ContractError` with `semantic.model` or the new `semantic.resolver`. A dotted path that names no Value raises `MissingReferenceError` with `by="path"` and the whole path in `requested`, not the Entity code. `Pivot.column` raises `MissingReferenceError` (a `LookupError`), not `KeyError`. Property content raises `DecodeError` and `EncodingError`; non-finite evaluation raises `NumericalError`; the rollup argument checks raise `RollupError`; MIN and MAX resampling and symbolic overdraft interest raise `UnsupportedError`. A `where` field names its owner by kind and code, for example `"Entity project"`, and `Specification.case` and `Outcome.case` report `kind="Specification"` and `kind="Outcome"`.
