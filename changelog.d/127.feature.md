`Outcome` reads its result: `outcome["land_cost"]` gives a solved Quantity (or a Flow) by key or dotted path, `outcome.quantity(reference)` reads by Reference, and `outcome.model` is the output Model. `outcome.status` is a new `rangekeeper.execution.Status` (`FEASIBLE`, `UNDERDETERMINED`, `INFEASIBLE`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `INVALID`, `LIMITED`, `FAILED`); `outcome.raise_for_status()` returns the Outcome or raises `UnsupportedError`, `ValidationError`, `NumericalError`, `AttemptDeadlineError`, `BackendUnavailableError` or the new `rangekeeper.execution.OutcomeError`. `outcome.free_unknowns` names the free values that the equations do not determine, and `outcome.evidence` gives the typed solver `Evidence`. The `underdetermined` diagnostic now references those undetermined free unknowns, not only the unknowns that no row constrains.
