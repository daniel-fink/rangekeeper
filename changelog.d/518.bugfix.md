`rk.finance.irr` and `rx.finance.irr` count an amount within `1e-8` of the gross amounts as zero when they check for a change of sign. A cash flow whose only inflows are rounding noise now raises `NumericalError` with the new code `no_sign_change`, and its message and `values` give the totals of the inflows and outflows. A solve reports the same code, also for a fixed IRR whose solved Flow has no real change of sign. A case that gave a rate near -100 % pa from noise now fails: read `error.code` to handle it.
