Added a one-off conversion tool, `python -m tools.upgrade`, in the repository (not in the wheel). It converts stored 0.x documents (Model 0.7.0 and 0.8.0, Specification 0.7.0, Run 0.4.0) and Bundles to the 1.0.0 formats: the version reset, Period and Span `start` and `end`, `policy` to `rules`, `outcomes` to `rule_evidence`, and currency magnitudes as decimal text. Documents keep their UUIDs. The upgrade guide explains when to rebuild and when to convert.
