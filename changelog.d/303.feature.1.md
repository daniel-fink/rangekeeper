Growth profiles take `start=` (zero before a date, or everywhere for `NEVER`) and `every=` (the amount once in each length of time, such as `"5 years"`); `Linear` and `Compounding` also take `base=`, the date of the initial amount. `Uniform` and `SCurve` take `start=` and `length=`: the window of Periods over which they divide the total. Lesson 03's rents, renewals and works use them.
