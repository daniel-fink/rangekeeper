A Specification fixes a date Value to a date or `rk.duration.NEVER`: `fix(start_date=date(2027, 1, 1))`. The stored Assignment has `date` or `never` in place of `quantity`, which is now optional. A solve settles the free dates that date equations determine, such as `a_start = shared_start + 1 year`, decides each equation over dates exactly (a false one makes the solve `INFEASIBLE`), records the dates in its output and verifies them; `outcome["a_open"]` gives the date.
