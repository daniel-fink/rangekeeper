`rk.finance.pv`, `npv`, `irr` and `payment` calculate with units on known Flows: `npv(flow=, rate="7 % pa", on=)` returns a Quantity, `irr(flow=)` returns a rate in `% pa`, and `payment(principal=, rate=, term=, frequency=)` is a spreadsheet's PMT with units. Period amounts are paid at the end of their Period, and the default day count is actual/actual (ISDA).
