`rk.io.bundle` is public, and the API snapshot freezes it with `rk.io.json`, `rk.io.yaml` and `rk.io.polars`. Each codec has an `__all__`, so its imported helpers are no longer public; `rk.io.atomic` is private. The options of `rk.io.bundle.write` and `Reader` are now values: `write(root, model, *, documents=, resolver=, geometry=GeometrySources(directories=, providers=), parquet=ParquetOptions(compression=, row_group_size=))` replaces `geometry=`, `geometries=`, `provider=`, `providers=`, `compression=` and `row_group_size=`, and `Reader(root, *, providers=, limits=ReadLimits(documents=, bytes=, rows=), index=IndexCheck.SCHEMA)` replaces `max_documents=`, `max_bytes=`, `max_rows=`, `check_index=False` and `provider=`. A pin with no provider in `providers` uses `rk.io.geometry_providers`. `rk.io.polars.to_frame` names its argument `value`, and `polars.dates(..., name="date")` raises `ContractError`. The Bundle format does not change.
