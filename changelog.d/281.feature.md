`rk.Model(name=, namespace=, description=)` makes an empty Model. `draft.add_entity(code=, classification=)` and `draft.entity(code=)` return entity handles, which declare values with `value(key=, units=)`, Flows with `flow(key=, units=, periods=)` and equations with `equations(...)`. With a namespace, the UUIDs that a draft makes are derived from it.
