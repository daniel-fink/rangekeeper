Walkthrough lesson 03, "Cases, debt and equity", is written: it builds the dated cash flows of the base plan, solves weak, reference and strong cases in one batch, funds the plan with equity first and then a loan, finds the land price of a target equity IRR, tests the funding, and executes and replays a Run. The walkthrough helpers gain `load_base_plan`, `plot_cash_flows` and `plot_flows`, and `show_timeline` takes `of=`, a plan. The mixed-use property data package gains `base_plan.json`, derived by `examples/data/derive_base_plan.py`.
