Flow-valued equations, in text and in `rx`: `"works = shared_works + a_works + b_works"` and `"loan_principal = rx.max(0 AUD, funding_need - equity)"` apply period by period, a value applies to every period, and the equations commit. Each equation is checked when it is written: Flows over other Periods do not combine (`ALIGNMENT_DIFFER`), and a Flow made from amounts, by a profile or `rx.flow.event`, needs the Flow that it defines to be declared with its Periods. `ValueHandle.periods` and `rx.Term.periods` give the declared Periods.
