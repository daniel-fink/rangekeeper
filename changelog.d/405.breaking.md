`ResampleMethod` has no `SPLIT` member: write `flow.split(frequency=...)` or `flow.split(periods=...)`, and `Stream.split`, to divide amounts over shorter Periods. `MeanWeighting` and every `weighting=` argument are removed (`Flow.resample`, `Stream.resample`, `FlowRollup` and `rangekeeper.model.formulation.flow.resample`): the weighting is part of the method, so write `ResampleMethod.MEAN` for `MeanWeighting.OBSERVATIONS` and the new `ResampleMethod.ELAPSED_MEAN` for `MeanWeighting.ELAPSED`. A mean no longer needs a second argument.
