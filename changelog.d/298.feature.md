Add `rangekeeper.market.Paths`, a stored, immutable document of market paths with its capture record. `paths[key][name]` gives the Flow of one series, `paths.mean(name=)` its mean, `Paths.from_flows(flows=, lag=)` makes paths from data, and `paths.regenerate(seed=)` makes them again with a registered path generator (`rangekeeper.market.generators`). Stores and Bundles hold the document (`load_paths(id)`), and `Specification.fix` takes a Flow for a Flow value.
