`rangekeeper.flow` defines its public types in modules without a leading underscore: `Flow` and `Movement` in `rangekeeper.flow.flow`, `Stream` in `rangekeeper.flow.stream`, the options in `rangekeeper.flow.options`, `FlowAggregate` in `rangekeeper.flow.aggregation` and `StreamTable` in `rangekeeper.flow.table`; `rangekeeper.flow` now also exports `StreamTable`, the type that `display()` returns. `pv`, `npv`, `irr` and `payment` are defined in `rangekeeper.finance.valuation`. Import paths from `rangekeeper.flow` and `rangekeeper.finance` do not change; code that imported the private modules `rangekeeper.flow._flow`, `_options` or `_table`, or `rangekeeper.finance._valuation`, uses the new modules (the codemod applies the moves).
