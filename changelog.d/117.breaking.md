Currency amounts are now exact decimals (ADR-023). A magnitude in ISO 4217 units, such as `AUD`, is a `Decimal` with at most 15 significant digits, and JSON, YAML and Bundle data store it as canonical decimal text: `"magnitude": "1250000.5"`. The JSON reader rejects a number or non-canonical text for an amount; the YAML reader also accepts a plain numeral. Use `.number` for float arithmetic: `Decimal` does not mix with `float`. `Settings.relative_tolerance` and `time_limit` are now `double`, `ImplementationKind` has the new value `convention`, and `minor_unit` moved from `rangekeeper.quantity` to `rangekeeper.money`. Documents written by 1.0.0a1 or earlier with numeric currency magnitudes must be converted.
