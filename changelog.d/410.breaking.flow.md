The public arguments of `flow`, `profile` and `finance` are keyword-only (D14): `Flow.from_periods(*, periods, magnitudes, units)`, `Flow.from_events(*, dates, magnitudes, units)`, `Stream.from_values(*, model, value_ids)`, and `calculate_pv`, `calculate_xnpv` and `calculate_irr` in `rangekeeper.finance.financial`, which take `flow=` like `rangekeeper.finance.pv`. The public records `FlowAggregate`, `StreamTable`, `IrrResult` and the profiles take their fields by name, for example `FlowAggregate(flow=, coverage=)` and `SCurve(distribution=)`. A positional call raises `TypeError`; name the arguments.
