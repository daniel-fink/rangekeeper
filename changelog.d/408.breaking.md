Every error takes its message first and each field by keyword, with the common fields `code`, `document_id`, `path` and `targets` (`rk.errors.ErrorFields`), and keeps all of its fields through `pickle`. `UnsupportedError` and `OutcomeError` take `document_id=` and `targets=` in place of `document=` and `target=`; `HierarchyError(code, message, ids=)` is now `HierarchyError(message, code=, targets=)`; `PublishedFileError(path, error)` is now `PublishedFileError(message, file=)`. A currency-amount rule of a record raises `ContractError` with its code, not a plain `ValueError`. `Issue.document_id` keeps its name, so stored diagnostics do not change.
