`rk.Quantity` takes text: `rk.Quantity(text="7 % pa")` is the same Quantity as `rk.Quantity(magnitude=7, units="% pa")`. `Quantity.to(units=)` converts to compatible units, and Quantities support unit-checked `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, negation and `abs`. A dimensionless operand such as `5 %` acts as its plain number, and units of the same dimension cancel, so `2 % pa * 1 year` is `0.02 dimensionless`.
