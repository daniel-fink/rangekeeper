A stored `Filter` or `Criterion` has `exclusive: true` or no `exclusive`; absent means that sub-types match. Validation refuses `exclusive: false`, `exclusive: null` and a Filter with `exclusive` but no `classification`. `python -m tools.upgrade documents --from 1.0.0a2` and `bundle --from 1.0.0a2` convert the documents and Bundles that 1.0.0a2 wrote: each Filter with a classification and each Criterion gets `exclusive: true`, so it keeps the exact match of 1.0.0a2. A 1.0.0a2 Bundle does not open without this conversion, because it pins the 1.0.0a2 slot map.
