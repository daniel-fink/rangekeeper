`rk.duration.Periods` is an ordered, gap-free sequence of Periods with one frequency, with `start`, `end`, `span`, `frequency`, `len`, indexing, `trim(span=)`, `extend(count=)`, `containing(on=)` and value equality. `Span(start=, length="10 years")` takes a length instead of an end, and `Span.extend(by="1 year")` returns a longer Span.
