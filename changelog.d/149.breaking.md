The unit registry is curated. It contains only ISO 4217 currencies, length, area and volume with SI prefixes, time without prefixes, count, dwelling, percent and ratio, and the aliases `sqm`, `sqft` and `pa` (per annum: `"AUD pa"` is AUD per year). Other Pint units, such as `kg`, `kWh`, `kelvin`, `degC` and prefixed time units, now raise `UnitError` with suggestions in `UnitError.suggestions`; `m2` and `ft2` are no longer aliases, so use `sqm` and `sqft`. `UnitSystem` adds `parse_quantity`, `minor_unit`, `register` and `alias`.
