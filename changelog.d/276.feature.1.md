Execution solves Flow-valued equations period by period, with the equation functions of the catalogue: profiles, `rx.flow.event`, `rx.finance.npv`, `rx.finance.interest` and the Flow methods become linear rows whose coefficients come from their value functions; `rx.finance.irr`, `x.min()` and `x.max()` are evaluated after the affine stage (D4), and `rx.max`, `rx.min`, `x.max()` and `x.min()` with free values take the active-side solve. Verification reads the largest term of a sum for its relative tolerance, because currency amounts have 15 significant digits.
