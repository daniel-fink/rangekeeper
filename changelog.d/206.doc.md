Walkthrough lesson 04, "Many market futures", is written: it carries the market in one rent index, builds a path from trend, cycle, volatility, noise and shock, generates and captures market paths with a seed, solves the plan across the paths, and reads the distribution of NPV and funding shortfall. It shows the flaw of averages through the share of paths with a loss. The walkthrough helpers gain `data_path`, `plot_paths` and `plot_target_curve`, and `plot_flows` takes `flows=`. The mixed-use property data package gains `03_base_plan.model.json` and `03_base_case.specification.json`, derived by `examples/data/derive_lesson_documents.py`.
