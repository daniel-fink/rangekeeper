The execution calls take keyword arguments only: `rk.execution.solve(specification=, resolver=, config=)`, `execute(specification=, store=, config=)`, `assemble(specification=, report=, id=, output=)`, `assemble_batch(specification=, cases=)`, `make_candidate(binding=, unknowns=, run_id=)` and `verify(binding=, candidate=, tolerances=)`. The records of `rk.execution`, `rk.execution.backends` and `rk.execution.rule` (`ExecutionConfig`, `Limits`, `Tolerances`, `Outcome`, `Binding`, `Cases`, `Validation`, `Assertion`, `AffineProblem`, `Row`, `Analysis`, `Evidence`, `Verification`, `Pair`, `BackendResult`, `Observation`, `RuleResult`) take keyword arguments only. Name each argument in calls that passed them by position.
