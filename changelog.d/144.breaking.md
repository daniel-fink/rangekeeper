`Specification.fix` and `free` no longer take `id=`, `model=`, `ids=`, `recorded=` or `resolver=`. Use `fix(key=quantity)` or `fix({target: quantity})` (a mapping of Movement UUIDs selects Movements) and `free(*targets)`; give a record its Model with `Specification(record, model=, resolver=)`. Fix recorded amounts explicitly instead of `recorded=True`.
