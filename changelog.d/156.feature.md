`Model.entity(code=)` and `Model.entity(name=)` return one Entity or Assembly. No match raises `MissingReferenceError`; several matches raise the new `rangekeeper.errors.AmbiguousReferenceError` (code `reference.ambiguous`). Both errors have the fields `requested`, `by`, `kind`, `where` and `candidates`, which say what was requested, where, and the nearest or matching codes.
