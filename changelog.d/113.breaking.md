`io.polars.to_frame(flow)` and `from_frame` are now a lossless round trip: integer magnitudes stay integers. The `magnitude` column is `Int64` when all values are integers, `Float64` when all are floats, and an `Object` column when they mix or exceed `Int64`; before, a mixed Flow became `Float64`. The Movement columns, the Model sections of `Update`, the closed Specification fields and the lineage slots of Metadata now come from the schema slots. The new `lineage` slot annotation marks `Metadata.id` and `Metadata.previous`; `slots.json` carries it for every slot.
