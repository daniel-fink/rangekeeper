`Reduction` splits into `QuantityRollup(key=, units=, reducer=)` and `FlowRollup(key=, reducer=, periods=, resampling=, ...)` in `rangekeeper.model.system.rollup`, with one `Reducer` enum (`SUM`, `MEAN`, `MIN`, `MAX`, `COUNT`) in place of the `reducers` module. Both select leaf contributors by default; a scalar `Reduction` used every node, so pass `contributors=Contributor.ALL` to keep that. Custom selectors and reducers are removed: select a Value by `key=` and optional `measure=`. `View.aggregate(reduction)` becomes `View.rollup(definition=)`, `AggregateEntry` becomes `RollupEntry` and `PivotColumn.aggregation` becomes `result`. The codemod lists each change.
