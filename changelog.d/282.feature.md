A value that an equation defines needs no declaration (D44): `project.equations("a_start = shared_start + 1 year", "income = rent * area")` defines `a_start` and `income`, and `commit()` works out their kind and units from the equations (a date; `AUD pa`). Declare the inputs and the results that you rely on; a declaration is checked against its equations. A key that is not declared and that no equation of the call defines still raises `EquationError` with the nearest keys.
