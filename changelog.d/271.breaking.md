The module `rangekeeper.model.characteristics` and its functions `value()` and `label()` are removed. Use `model.value(owner=entity.id, key=)` and the new `model.label(owner=entity.id, key=)`; `Model.label(id)` also looks up a Label by UUID. A missing key now raises `MissingReferenceError`, with the owner and the nearest keys, instead of returning `None`.
