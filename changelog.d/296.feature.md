A batch `Outcome` gives the distribution of one value across its cases with `distribution(key=)`: a `rangekeeper.execution.Distribution` of a scalar value, with `summary()`, `mean()`, `standard_deviation()`, `quantile(level=)`, `probability(below=, above=)`, `counts()` and `case(quantile=)`, or a `DateDistribution` of a date value, with `counts()`. `Outcome.mean()` gives the `Mean` of a batch, which `rangekeeper.execution.compare` takes as one leaf Outcome. A Flow value, or the mean of a date value, raises `ContractError` with the new code `distribution.value`.
