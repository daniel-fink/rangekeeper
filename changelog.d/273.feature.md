`rk.flow.Flow(amounts={period_or_date: amount}, units=)` makes a Flow from a mapping; with Quantities as amounts the units come from them. `Flow.at(on=)` returns the amount on a date. Flows support `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and negation with numbers, Quantities and other Flows. A Stream gives one Flow with `stream[name]`, adds lines with `with_flows(flows=)`, and its `sum()` adds lines with different coordinates and dates period amounts when the lines mix dated and period Flows. Periods and Spans are hashable.
