`rangekeeper.execution.ExecutionConfig` holds the backend, unit system, default limits, verification tolerances and backend options of an execution, and is the one source of their defaults. `Executor` takes `config=ExecutionConfig(...)` in place of its `backend=`, `units=` and `tolerances=` arguments. `Limits` is public and refuses a value that is not positive and finite. A backend's `solve` receives the `options`; the `pyomo-highs` backend passes them to HiGHS.
