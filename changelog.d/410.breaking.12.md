`rk.io` and the extension registries take keyword-only arguments (D14): `rk.io.json` and `rk.io.yaml` `loads(text=, kind=)`, `read(path=, kind=)` and `write(document=, path=)`; `rk.io.bundle.write(root=, model=, ...)` and `Reader(root=, ...)`; `rk.io.polars.dates(flow=, ...)` and `from_frame(frame=, units=)`; `GeometryProvider.validate(path=, geometry=)`, so a provider declares `def validate(self, *, path, geometry)`; and `register(name=, implementation=)` on `rk.quantity.unit_systems`, `rk.io.geometry_providers`, `rk.profile.profiles` and `rk.execution.backends.registry`. Name the arguments that you passed by position. The store protocol (`put(document)`, `load_model(id)`) stays positional.
