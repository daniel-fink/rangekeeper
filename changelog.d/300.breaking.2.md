The dated rule slice is gone: the records `RuleSet`, `Decision`, the old `Rule`, `Action`, `ActionKind`, `ObservationBinding`, `ObservedQuantity` and `DecisionEvidence`, `Report.decision_evidence`, and `rangekeeper.execution.rule.evaluate`, `observe`, `RuleResult` and `RuleCapabilityError`. A rule is a query over a window, and `rk.execution.solve` runs it. `rangekeeper.schema.records.Rule` is now the stored form of that query, in `Specification.rules`.
