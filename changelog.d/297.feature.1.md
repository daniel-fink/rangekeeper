A `Paired` comparison gives `distribution(outcome=, key=)`, the `Distribution` of one Outcome's paired differences, and puts its pairs in `rangekeeper.execution.Groups` with `by(key=)`, by the value of each compared case at a key, such as a start date, or with `by_case`, by case. `Groups.summary()` gives the statistics of each group. `Paired.summary()` now has the columns of `Distribution.summary()`: it adds `standard_deviation`, `quantile_05` and `quantile_95` to `count`, `mean`, `minimum`, `maximum` and `share_above_zero`; read its cells by column name.
