Names that were public by accident are private: only a draft makes `EntityHandle` and `ValueHandle` (calling either raises `TypeError`), and `rangekeeper.model.system` no longer lists the `matching` and `rollup` modules; import `Match`, the rollup classes and the new export `QuantityPopulation` from `rangekeeper.model.system`. `View.quantities` returns the documented `QuantityPopulation`, now keyword-only. `ValueHandle` has a `kind`, and both handles display briefly without UUIDs, for example `<value land_cost [AUD] on project>`.
