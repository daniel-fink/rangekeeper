`rk.duration` raises Rangekeeper errors with codes. `Periods.trim` across a Period, `Span.periods` with a partial final Period and `Periods.extend` after one raise the new `rk.duration.AlignmentError`, a `ContractError` with `Code.ALIGNMENT_CROSSES` or `Code.ALIGNMENT_PARTIAL` and the Periods in `values`. A negative count, a slice of `Periods` with a step, an empty `cover` and a step outside the years 1 to 9999 raise `ContractError`. Each is a `ValueError`, so `except ValueError` still works. A count that is not an `int`, such as `True` or `2.0`, now raises `TypeError`, not `ValueError`.
