Rollup and pivot failures use the rollup word (D38): `rangekeeper.model.system.AggregationError` is `RollupError`, and its code `rk.errors.Code.AGGREGATION` (`"aggregation"`) is `Code.ROLLUP` (`"rollup"`). `aggregate` stays the word for combining Flows.
