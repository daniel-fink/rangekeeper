`UnsupportedError` gains `equation`, the text of the failing Constraint (for example `"noi = coverage * debt_service"`), and `unknowns`, the keys of the unknowns in a term that is not affine. A failed Run's diagnostic keeps both in its `values`, and each `constraint_residual` diagnostic gives the `equation` that it checked. `Outcome.raise_for_status()` raises the `UnsupportedError` with both fields.
