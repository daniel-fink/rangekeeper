The backend contract is backend-neutral. A backend returns `rangekeeper.execution.backends.BackendResult` (was `Result`) with a `Termination` (`FEASIBLE`, `INFEASIBLE`, `LIMIT`, `ERROR` or `UNKNOWN`) in place of a backend status word; the `solver_termination` diagnostic records that value as `termination` and the Pyomo condition as `termination_condition`. `AffineProblem` has `variables`, unscaled rows with a `Relation`, and an `Analysis` of the equality rank and free variables, which rk computes for every backend without NumPy. The HiGHS row-scaling range and iteration-limit clamp now live in the `pyomo-highs` backend.
