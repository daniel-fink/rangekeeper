Add `rangekeeper.market.Dynamics` and `rangekeeper.market.generate(dynamics=, periods=, seed=, count=)`: declare a trend, a cycle, volatility, noise and a shock for each named series, each as Quantity text or a distribution, and make independent market paths as a `Paths` document. Random dynamics need a seed, and the error names the random components; fixed dynamics take none. The built-in path generator `dynamics` is registered in `rangekeeper.market.generators`, so `paths.regenerate(seed=)` makes the paths again.
