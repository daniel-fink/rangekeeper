The function catalogue for equations, in text and in `rx`: `rx.profile.compounding`, `linear`, `constant`, `uniform` and `scurve` (and every profile that an extension registers, under its name), `rx.flow.event(amount=, on=)`, `rx.finance.npv(flow=, rate=, on=)`, `rx.finance.irr(flow=)` and `rx.finance.interest(balance=, rate=)`, and the Flow methods `cumulative()`, `diff()`, `since(on=)`, `until(on=)`, `first()`, `last()`, `min()` and `max()`. Each mirrors its value function; `rk.finance.interest(balance=, rate=, day_count=)` is new and gives the interest on a balance in each Period at an effective rate per year.
