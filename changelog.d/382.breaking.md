One rule names a Value by its key in the whole Model: `Model.value(key=)` reads a key, a dotted key (`building_a.income`) or the text of a Value UUID, and the new `Model.key_of(id)` gives the key that names a Value or Movement. A Value whose key is not unique and whose owner has no unique Entity or Assembly code, such as a Relationship Value, is named by its UUID text, not by `<owner UUID>.key`, which did not resolve. `Specification.roles`, `AmbiguousReferenceError.candidates`, `Outcome.free_unknowns`, `UnsupportedError.unknowns`, the stored `values["unknowns"]` and the equation text of a `nonaffine_unknowns` diagnostic use it, so two Entities with `noi` give `a.noi` and `b.noi`, not `noi` twice. A `nonaffine_unknowns` diagnostic now references its unknowns, as `underdetermined` does.
