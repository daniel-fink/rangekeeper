`rk.execution.compare` returns a `SideBySide` without `baseline` and a `Paired` with one, in place of one `Comparison` with two modes. `SideBySide` has `values` and `select`; `Paired` has `compared`, `pairs`, `difference`, `differences`, `summary`, `select` and `where`. Neither has a public constructor. The call and its arguments do not change.
