Equations have keys: a draft names each equation by the key of the first value of its text. Entity handles edit equations by key with `replace(key=, equation=)`, `add_to(key=, term=)` and `remove(key=)`, and `draft.remove(entity=)` removes an entity. `model.equations` lists the equations of a revision as `Equation` values, with `key`, `keys`, `text`, `formulation` and `entity`, and `where(key=, keys=, formulation=, entity=)` selects them. `MissingReferenceError.referenced_by` names the equations that still use a removed value.
