Rule evaluation moves from `rangekeeper.specification.rule` to the new `rangekeeper.execution.rule`. Import `evaluate`, `observe`, `Observation`, `RuleResult` and `RuleCapabilityError` from `rangekeeper.execution.rule`. `rangekeeper.specification.rule` keeps only the rule declarations (`RuleSet`, `Decision`, `Rule`, `Action`, `ActionKind`, `ObservationBinding`, `ObservedQuantity` and `RuleEvidence`) and their validation. The codemod rewrites these imports.
