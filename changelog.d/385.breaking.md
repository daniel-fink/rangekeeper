The `ValueRelation` comparisons use the words of the expression `Operator`: `less_than`, `less_than_or_equal`, `greater_than` and `greater_than_or_equal` (were `less`, `less_or_equal`, `greater` and `greater_or_equal`). `Match.missing` is `Match.undecided`, the word of `Coverage.undecided`; `ValueRelation.MISSING` keeps its name. Both are new since 1.0.0a2, so no stored document of a release changes.
