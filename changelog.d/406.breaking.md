Flow and Stream methods take no flag or mode-only arguments. `flow.check(resolved=True)` is `flow.require_resolved()`; `clean(remove_zeroes=True)` is `clean().drop_zeroes()`; `extent()` takes no flag; `collapse(on=)` takes a date or a `PeriodTiming` (was `timing=`); `integrate(day_count=)` is `integrate_time(day_count=)`; `from_periods` and `from_events` take no `dates=` or `ids=` (make the `Movement` records); `multiply` and `scale` follow `*`, so an unresolved amount gives an unresolved product. `Stream.select(value_ids=)` is `Stream.select_values(ids)`. A Stream's `missing` and `join` policies are set on the Stream (`Stream(..., missing=, join=)` or `stream.with_policy(...)`): `aggregate`, `sum`, `min`, `max` and `resample` take no `missing=` or `join=`, and `sum(timing=)` is `stream.dated(timing=).sum()`. `total` and `collapse` accept `MissingValueHandling.ZERO`.
